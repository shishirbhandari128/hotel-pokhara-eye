Our Story

Himalayan Warmth, Contemporary Comfort

Hotel Pokhara Eye was born from a simple idea: give every guest an unobstructed sunrise over the Annapurnas without ever leaving their room. Set on a quiet ridge above the noise of Lakeside, yet only minutes from Phewa Lake's cafés and boats, the hotel blends authentic Nepali hospitality with the quiet polish of modern boutique design.

Locally sourced textiles, hand-finished woodwork, and a team that treats every arrival like family — this is a place to slow down, breathe alpine air, and watch the mountains change color from dawn to dusk.

  • Prime ridge-top location with uninterrupted mountain views
  • Peaceful, away from traffic noise — yet steps from Lakeside Pokhara
  • Genuine Nepali hospitality with contemporary comfort
